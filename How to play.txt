CENTIPEDE - HOW TO PLAY
=======================

OBJECTIVE:
- Destroy the centipede and other enemies while avoiding collisions
- Score as many points as possible
- Progress through increasingly difficult levels

CONTROLS:
---------
MOVEMENT:
  W / Up Arrow    - Move Up
  A / Left Arrow  - Move Left
  S / Down Arrow  - Move Down
  D / Right Arrow - Move Right

COMBAT:
  Spacebar - Shoot Bullets

GAME ELEMENTS:
--------------
PLAYER (Spaceship):
  - Controlled by you
  - Can move freely in the lower portion of the screen
  - Can shoot bullets upward

CENTIPEDE:
  - Main enemy that moves across the screen
  - Splits into segments when hit
  - Changes direction when hitting mushrooms or screen edges
  - Head segment is worth more points

MUSHROOMS:
  - Obstacles that block movement and centipede path
  - Can be destroyed by shooting them 2 times
  - Appear when centipede segments are destroyed

SPIDER:
  - Moves randomly around the screen
  - Points vary based on distance from player when killed:
    * Close range (≤2 boxes): 900 points
    * Medium range (≤3 boxes): 600 points
    * Long range: 300 points
  - Can destroy mushrooms by moving over them

SCORPION:
  - Moves horizontally across the screen
  - Worth 1000 points when destroyed
  - Poisons mushrooms it touches (poisoned mushrooms are lethal!)

POISONED MUSHROOMS:
  - Created when scorpion passes over regular mushrooms
  - Instantly kill player on contact
  - Visually different from regular mushrooms

SCORING SYSTEM:
---------------
- Centipede body segment: 10 points
- Centipede head segment: 20 points
- Mushroom destruction: 1 point
- Spider: 300-900 points (based on distance)
- Scorpion: 1000 points

GAME RULES:
-----------
1. Player starts with limited movement area (cannot go above row 25)
2. Only one bullet can be on screen at a time
3. Game ends if player collides with:
   - Any centipede segment
   - Spider
   - Scorpion
   - Poisoned mushroom

LEVEL PROGRESSION:
------------------
- Advance to next level when all centipede segments are destroyed
- Each new level:
  * Centipede moves faster
  * More mushrooms appear
  * Centipede gains additional segments
  * Enemies respawn

TIPS & STRATEGIES:
------------------
1. Prioritize destroying the centipede head first
2. Create safe paths by destroying mushrooms strategically
3. Watch for poisoned mushrooms - they're instant death!
4. Kill spiders when they're far away for maximum points
5. Scorpions are high-value targets but dangerous
6. Use the environment to trap the centipede
7. Keep moving to avoid enemy collisions

HIGH SCORES:
------------
- Your highest score is saved automatically
- Compete to beat your personal best!

GOOD LUCK AND HAVE FUN!
